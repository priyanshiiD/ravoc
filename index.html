Meet ROVAC™

ROVAC is an autonomous floor-cleaning robot that combines cutting-edge sensing technology with intelligent navigation to deliver hands-free, systematic cleaning of your entire home every single day.

At its core, ROVAC uses a 360° rotating LiDAR sensor to map your environment in real-time, plan optimal cleaning paths, detect and avoid obstacles, and automatically return to its charging dock when the job is done.

How ROVAC Works

Every cleaning cycle follows the same intelligent sequence. Watch the full guided video explanation or scroll down to explore each stage interactively.

01
Scan Environment
ROVAC's rotating LiDAR sensor fires infrared beams in all directions, measuring distances to walls, furniture, and obstacles in real-time.
See it live → LiDAR section
02
Build Map
As scan data accumulates, ROVAC builds a 2D map of the room -- marking free space, walls, and obstacles -- forming the basis for navigation.
See it live → LiDAR section
03
Navigate
Using the room map, ROVAC plans a systematic boustrophedon (S-pattern) path designed to cover the floor efficiently with minimal overlap.
See it live → Navigation section
04
Clean
As it moves along each path, four cleaning stages work simultaneously: side brush, main roller, suction channel, and HEPA filtration.
See it live → Cleaning section
05
Avoid Obstacles
Infrared proximity sensors detect unexpected objects in real-time. ROVAC slows, calculates a new route, and continues without stopping.
See it live → Avoidance section
06
Return & Recharge
When the job is done or battery is low, ROVAC autonomously locates its dock, navigates back, aligns precisely, and begins recharging.

Cleaning System

Four-Stage Debris Removal

ROVAC uses a coordinated four-stage process -- from edge sweeping to HEPA filtration -- to capture every particle on your floor, including fine dust, pet hair, and embedded debris.

01
Side Brush
The angled side brush sweeps debris from corners and edges toward the main intake path, reaching where the body cannot.
02
Roller Brush
The dual-action roller brush agitates carpet fibers and pulls debris off hard floors into the suction channel.
03
Suction
High-powered suction draws all loosened debris upward through the intake channel at speed.
04
HEPA Collection
Debris passes through a HEPA filter that traps fine particles before air exits the system, and is stored in the sealed dustbin.
